# Questionnaire — CISCSR public website content (EN)

**Subject:** CISCSR website — public content (HTML pages)

---

We are preparing the evolution of the Confederation’s website into a multipage static HTML site.  
Chat, private members’ areas, and a shop are deferred to a later phase.  
We ask you to validate the points below so that “In preparation” pages can be replaced with official content.

**Reference:** planning docs in `Docs/site-evolution/` (inventory, information architecture, research note on the Constitutions of 1762 and 1786).  
**Current site:** https://pfigueiredo.github.io/CICSR_REAA/

## Questions

1. **Institutional document**  
   Do you confirm that the public Confederation text to publish is the **Internal Regulations** (Lisbon, 3 July 2026)? Is there a public **Charter / Statutes** in addition to the Regulations?

2. **Presidency**  
   Which Supreme Council / person currently holds the Presidency? What are the mandate dates?  
   (Regulations: rotation Portugal → Morocco → Brazil → France, three-year terms.)

3. **Communications**  
   May we publish a **President’s Address**?  
   If yes, please send: full text · available languages · photo credit (if applicable).  
   What is the policy for previous presidents’ addresses?

4. **Events**  
   Please provide a list of **public** past and upcoming events (date, place, type / short description).  
   May we already list: Lyon, 12 December 2025 (treaty signing) and Lisbon, 3 July 2026 (Regulations / Assembly)?

5. **Contacts**  
   Institutional email, postal address, telephone (if any).  
   Are there public ambassadors / representatives for each member Supreme Council?

6. **Members**  
   Are there members beyond the four founders to present in this phase?  
   What is the public order of the founders (current site list vs presidential rotation order)?  
   Should the names of the Sovereign Grand Commanders appear on the public member cards?

7. **Spelling — Morocco**  
   Please confirm the public form **Kamal El Fadhi** (and in Arabic **كمال الفاضي**, or indicate the preferred form).

8. **Constitutions of 1762 and 1786**  
   Preferred treatment on the site:  
   - **(A)** Institutional summary only  
   - **(B)** Summary + link to an approved historical edition (e.g. Gallica / Internet Archive)  
   - **(C)** PDF hosted on the site (specify the exact file)

9. **If (B) or (C)**  
   Provide the approved URL or file; languages (FR / EN / PT / other).  
   Do you authorise a public note stating that several historical versions exist and that the link is not an official CISCSR edition?

10. **History of the A.A.S.R.**  
    Do you authorise a brief mention of Charleston 1801 (already in the Declaration of Principles) and a minimal expansion of the chronology, without debates on the authenticity of 1786?

11. **Bulletin**  
    When a digital bulletin exists, should it be published under **Communications**?
